# Probes for the review of 2026-10-10, for v13, and for doc hygiene

Scripts behind every number of the review in `NOTES.md` §15, and of the dependencies of v13 in §17. They are not checks:
CI does not run them, and no result rests on them. Run them with `python3 probes/review/<probe>` from the repository's
root.

| Probe | For | What it showed (2026-10-10) |
|---|---|---|
| `faithful_optimizers.py` | §15, point 1 | optimizers of the right objective, with no proxy, are charged: vanilla policy gradient on `F` leaves a median misaligned share of `0.16` up to step 1,600, best-of-2 `0.15`, best-of-16 `0.036`; under the ordinal specification ([P36]), policy gradient's median share is `0.004`. Ten seconds |
| `known_groups.py` | §15, point 1 | with a proxy that correlates about `0.96` with the target, the misaligned share ranks the exact pursuit of the proxy below best-of-4 on the target in 65% of pairs (AUC `0.35`); the ordinal share separates them (AUC `0.98` to `1.00`). Ten seconds |
| `library_edges.py` | §15, points 3 to 5 | `infinite`: `stdalign` returns `M = ∞` for an actor of full support with mass `10⁻¹²` off the best outcome; `boundary`: the χ² reference of `from_counts` rejects an actor at the default in up to `10.3%` of samples at a nominal 5%, and the χ̄² mixture does not; `counts`: the counts interval covers `94.0%` to `96.5%`; `log-ratios`: the log-ratio interval covers `84%` to `87%` at 32 draws near the ray, and `46%` far from it. About ten minutes in all |
| `premise_reach.py` | §17, §18 | which results use each imported axiom, background condition and premise of v13, by citation: none cites one in its Statement or Proof; through [D3]'s Statement, which cites [A5] for the word "declared", 16 results reach [A5], and no proof uses that word. Through the reasons, statuses and interpretations of the core, 38 results reach [A4] and [A5], 13 reach [A1]. A second (2026-10-11); rerun after Q53 (§18), 68 results: still none cites a premise, [M2] is cited by [P55], and 40 results reach each premise through the core's reasons, statuses and interpretations |
| `doc_sweep.py` | §19 | the doc sweep of 2026-10-11: paths and links that name nothing, counts that disagree with lint, American spellings, long lines, whitespace and broken paragraphs, in every Markdown source but the generated ones. | `p55_dependence.py` | §19, the reread | what [P55]'s test certifies when decisions are not independent: an actor that copies its first decision, with an intended average, is accused at every record of 8 decisions or more; a Markov chain with that average, keeping its outcome with probability `0.9`, in `17.9%` of records of 200 at `α = 0.05`; independent decisions that drift around it in none of 20,000. A few seconds |
| `q81_plugin_bias.py` | §39, issue 3 and H71 | on simulated data with case M1's shape (400 conditions, limit `0.10`, overall breach rate `0.326`): an actor with the same rate in every condition has misalignment `0.190` against the overall limit, and its plug-in from 4 draws per condition averages `0.220`; with 1 draw per condition the plug-in is about `0.385` whatever the truth, so a record that repeats no condition leaves `[0.190, 0.385]` open. And an edge of the library: `overall_rate_limit` returns `∞` when one condition's breach rate is `10⁻¹⁷`, where the value at `0` is `0.542`. Ten seconds; outputs in `RESULTS-q81.md` (2026-11-10) |
| `q81_universal_bet.py` | §39, H69 | on 25 records of 30 to 3,000 decisions (independent, a Markov chain, fixed blocks, every breach at the end), the log-wealth of one mixture bet fixed in advance lies below `n·M_A` of the record's shares by `4.8` to `19.0`, never by more than `0.76` of `d·log(n + 1) + 1`, the bound §39 proves. A test of the inequality on instances; the proof is in §39. A minute; outputs in `RESULTS-q81.md` (2026-11-11) |
| `q82_thresholds.py` | §40.1 | the threshold of [P55]'s test from counts against the one §39.3 gives the same counts under any dependence, at `α = 0.05`: `16.1` against `24.7` nats for 3 outcomes and 1,000 decisions, the ratio between `1.3` and `1.8` on six sizes. Instant (2026-11-11) |
