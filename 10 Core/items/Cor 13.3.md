## Statement

**Corollary 13.3 (rescaling is purely axial).** *[Assumes (E).]* For `F̂ = sF`, `s > 0`: `D_⊥ = X_anti = 0`, `t̂ = sβ`, and
`β·R_J = D_∥ = A(β) − A(sβ) + (s − 1)βA'(sβ) > 0` for `s ≠ 1`.
