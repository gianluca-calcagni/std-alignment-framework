# B3 — An objective up to scale and constant
> [!info] Generated from [CORE.md](https://github.com/gianluca-calcagni/std-alignment-framework/blob/main/CORE.md#b3--an-objective-up-to-scale-and-constant). Edit the source, not this note.

## Statement
An objective `F` and `a·F + c`, for numbers `a > 0` and `c`, state the same aim; the units of `F` matter
only to the stakes.

## In plain terms
Doubling every score, or adding the same amount to all of them, does not change what is asked for.

## Status
A background condition, assumed without loss of generality: pursuit, every specification built from `F`,
and misalignment are unchanged by the change; the stakes are reported in the declared units, and scale with `a`.

## Why this choice
A pursuit reveals its objective only up to a positive factor and a constant ([[P1 — Every behaviour is a tilt of any other|P1]](ii)), so no
behaviour can tell the two statements apart. Fixing one of them loses nothing a record could show; the units return
where the principal needs them, in the stakes (section 5).
