# B4 — One actor and one principal
> [!info] Generated from [CORE.md](https://github.com/gianluca-calcagni/std-alignment-framework/blob/main/CORE.md#b4--one-actor-and-one-principal). Edit the source, not this note.

## Statement
Several actors acting on joint outcomes count as one actor, and several principals with declared weights
count as one principal.

## In plain terms
A team is judged as one player on what the team does, and a committee with agreed weights as one
judge.

## Status
A background condition, assumed without loss of generality once the joint outcomes and the weights are
declared. How a group reaches its joint behaviour, and how the weights are chosen, are out of scope (section 0).

## Why this choice
On joint outcomes, the coordination of a group is a measured quantity rather than an assumption
([[P39 — Several actors: coordination plus individual misalignment|P39]]), and several principals with declared weights pool exactly into one ([[P42 — Several principals: gridlock, and the pooled pursuit|P42]]).

## Lineage
