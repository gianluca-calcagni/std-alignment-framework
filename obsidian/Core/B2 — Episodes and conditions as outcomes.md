# B2 — Episodes and conditions as outcomes
> [!info] Generated from [CORE.md](https://github.com/gianluca-calcagni/std-alignment-framework/blob/main/CORE.md#b2--episodes-and-conditions-as-outcomes). Edit the source, not this note.

## Statement
Decisions that depend on each other within an episode count as one outcome, the episode; and a situation
the actor does not choose counts as part of the outcome, with its frequency fixed.

## In plain terms
A whole conversation, game or treatment can be one outcome, and the situation it happened in can be
part of it.

## Status
A background condition, assumed without loss of generality: episodes, and pairs of a situation and an
outcome, are again finitely many outcomes ([[B1 — Finitely many outcomes|B1]]), with a behaviour on them. It is how a principal who intends decisions
to depend on each other says so: within an episode they may; between episodes, the accepted behaviours decide
independently, a convention of the declaration (the table of conventions above).

## Why this choice
A record of dependent decisions grouped into episodes, or of situations with their frequencies, is
again a record of outcomes on a finite set, so every result applies to it unchanged; results about several situations
are stated on pairs of a situation and an outcome (`derived/estimation.md`). One long record with no episodes is not
covered (section 0, out of scope).
