# Diagnostics: what misalignment is made of

Results that take a measured misalignment apart, so that a test can ask where it comes from: the intensity at which it
is judged ([[P43 — Misalignment at any intensity|P43]]), the other objectives the actor may be pursuing ([[P44 — What named objectives explain|P44]]), the runs of one procedure that differ by
chance ([[P45 — Drift: what runs share, and what they do not|P45]]), and what runs share between two conditions, such as evaluation and use ([[P46 — What runs share between conditions, and what they do not|P46]]); a target known only to
lie in a family ([[P48 — Misalignment when the target is uncertain|P48]]); how training on an evaluator splits misalignment into outer and inner parts ([[P49 — Outer and inner misalignment|P49]]); and how
much of a change acts on the measurement rather than on the world, and what signals, audits and re-measurements reveal
of it ([[P50 — Tampering: a change of the measurement, not of the world|P50]], [[P51 — What signals, audits and re-measurements reveal of tampering|P51]]). One result says what the estimates cost in samples ([[P47 — The cost of reweighting|P47]]). Each follows from earlier results;
they were derived after the first tests on data not seen before (`NOTES.md` §6), to design the next ones.
