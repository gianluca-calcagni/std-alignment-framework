# Derived — tilts and paths

Every full-support behaviour is a tilt of any other, so describing behaviour as a reweighted default loses nothing
([[P1 — Every behaviour is a tilt of any other|P1]]). Every change of behaviour follows a replicator equation, which is the steepest climb of [[A2 — Pursuit is the steepest climb|A2]] ([[P2 — Every change of behaviour follows a replicator equation|P2]]). Whether a
change keeps one objective can be read from the change alone ([[P3 — A fixed objective is visible in the changes of behaviour|P3]]).
