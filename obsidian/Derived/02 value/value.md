# Derived — value

What the KL divergence measures: the net value lost against a pursuit ([[P4 — What KL measures|P4]]). And why the cost of departing from the
default is KL: it is the only cost for which pursuit, the steepest climb of [[A2 — Pursuit is the steepest climb|A2]], is also the best trade-off of [[A3 — Pursuit is the best trade-off|A3]]
([[P14 — The cost of departing from the default is forced|P14]]). The identity of [[P4 — What KL measures|P4]] has a counterpart for any convex cost and any concave value, with a Bregman divergence in
place of KL ([[P38 — Any convex cost|P38]]).
