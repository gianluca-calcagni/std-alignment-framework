# Derived — feasibility

When the actor's limits are linear, misalignment splits exactly into what the actor could have avoided and what it
could not ([[P15 — Misalignment splits into what the actor could avoid and what it could not|P15]]). When the limit is a budget on how far the actor may depart from the default, the best it can do is
to pursue and stop where the budget runs out, and the budget acts as a price ([[P27 — The best use of a departure budget|P27]]); the width of the budget along a
function is how far the budget lets that function's average move ([[P28 — The width of a departure budget|P28]]). Budgets of other shapes control other
measures of a function's size, and a KL budget reaches rare outcomes cheaply ([[P31 — Budgets of other shapes|P31]]). An actor that must counter
situations it does not choose spends departure to do so, which puts a floor under the variety of the result ([[P32 — Regulation costs departure|P32]]).
