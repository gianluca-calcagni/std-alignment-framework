# Derived — identifiability

A single behaviour reveals an objective only relative to a default ([[P1 — Every behaviour is a tilt of any other|P1]]). Changes of behaviour reveal more: their
objective needs no default ([[P2 — Every change of behaviour follows a replicator equation|P2]]), and whether it stays fixed is testable ([[P3 — A fixed objective is visible in the changes of behaviour|P3]]). This file adds how much of a change
is misaligned, read from its first step ([[P11 — The misaligned share at the start of a change|P11]]); what interventions reveal about how an actor responds ([[P12 — What interventions reveal|P12]]); how
much of the objective a change gains ([[P13 — What the start of a change gains|P13]]); and what an actor can hide in conditions that are not observed, given
what it can perceive ([[P16 — An actor cannot behave more differently than it can tell conditions apart|P16]], [[P17 — What an unobserved condition can hide|P17]]). Each result also says what cannot be revealed.
