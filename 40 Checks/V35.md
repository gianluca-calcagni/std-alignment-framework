## Test design

The labels P1–P10, D1 and X1–X3 refer to [[R7-7 preregistration]] (sha256 `21170433…`), committed before any R7-7
computation. The generator, the tolerances and the solver starts are the registered ones. Each line says whether
its prediction held; the four that failed as registered (P6, P7, P8, P10) are diagnosed in [[R7-7 results]], with
the post-hoc script `70 Project/R7/r77_diagnose.py`, which regenerates exactly these instances.

- The ordinal free measure and its projection are computed in closed form (Prop. [[Prop 32|32]](b)), on the levels of `F`,
  so tied values are handled. The generic optimizers (P1) and the ordinal budget measure (P7, P8, D1) use SLSQP in
  level coordinates: a positive first ratio and non-negative increments.
- The ordinal budget measure is the minimum over five starts: the cardinal budget point, and four random
  monotone ratios; `p̂` itself is never a start. A value is kept only if its end point satisfies both constraints
  to `10⁻⁹`.
- Lines that come from the budget solver (P7's budget maximum, P8, D1, X1) depend on a non-convex optimizer. They
  were rerun on a second SIMD path before this output was recorded ([[R7-7 results]]).
