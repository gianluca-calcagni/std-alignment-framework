# T7 case 1c — amendment to [[T7-1b preregistration]] (before any Elo value is read)

**Why.** T7-1b's D2 fired: only 18 matched models have a value in `Arena Elo [April 18, 2024]`, fewer than the 25
required, so P4 and P5 were not evaluated. I chose the April column as "the most recent" without checking its
coverage; that was the error. **Seen since T7-1b:** P1–P3's results (P1, P2 hold; P3 fails, `ρ = 0.083`), and, for each
Elo column, only the *count* of matched models with a value: February 37, April 19 (one fewer after the integrity check).
No Elo value has been read.

**Amendment.** P4 and P5 are evaluated exactly as registered in T7-1b, with `Arena Elo [Feb 2, 2024]` in place of the
April column. Nothing else changes: same models, bins, reference, thresholds, decision rules. P1–P3 stand as already
recorded; they do not depend on Elo.

**Honesty note.** Choosing the column after D2 fired is a decision made after seeing coverage, not values. It is
recorded here so that a reader can discount it.
