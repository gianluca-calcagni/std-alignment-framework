### C2 — The width is the exact worst case (Thm [[Thm 5|5]], Lemma [[Lemma 5.1|5.1]], Prop. [[Prop 6|6]])
**Asserts.** Under a KL capacity `δ`, `R^C ≤ w_δ(E)` for every actor, with equality in the supremum over
intents for the pure capacity maximizer. The width has a Donsker–Varadhan formula and known small- and
large-`δ` asymptotes.
**Breaks if.** The KKT argument in Lemma [[Lemma 5.1|5.1]] fails, or the attaining construction in Thm [[Thm 5|5]](ii) does.
**Status.** Proved; [[V04|V4]] (0 / 3,000 violations; attainment `c = 0.999` exactly).
