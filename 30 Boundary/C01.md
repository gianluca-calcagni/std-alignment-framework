### C1 — The regret identity ([[Core index|Core]] Thm [[Thm 1|1]], Cors [[Cor 1.1|1.1]]–[[Cor 1.4|1.4]])
**Asserts.** `β·R_J = KL(p̂‖p*)`; the v5 optimality gap is `(1/β)×` the Jeffreys divergence; CGF and
integral forms; the ratio of the two is a weighted mean of `t/β`.
**Breaks if.** Algebra error.
**Cheapest route.** By hand — three lines.
**Status.** Proved; [[V01|V1]] agreement to 10⁻¹³. Extended in v6.1:
- Prop. [[Prop 15|15]] — any convex regularizer and concave target; regret is a Bregman divergence ([[V12]]);
- Cor. [[Cor 1.5|1.5]] — stacked stages add in the exponent ([[V15]]).

Both are proved. Theorem [[Thm 1|1]] itself is known (Korbak et al. 2022).
