### C6 — What optimization pressure does (Prop. [[Prop 14|14]])
**Asserts.** For the entropic actor, the initial sign of the effect of optimization is `−Cov_q(F̂,F)`. For
any smooth optimizer, it is the covariance in that optimizer's geometry (Prop. [[Prop 22|22]]). The terminal value is set
by argmax agreement, and the raw alignment regret has no sign.
**Status.** Proved; [[V08|V8]]. The measured frequencies are generator-dependent and marked so.
