# Archive note — the Alignment Subframework

The six files beside this note are the **Alignment Subframework**, supplied by the PI after v7.4 and archived
unedited (the `__MACOSX` resource forks in the original zip were dropped). It is the source of retraction rows
1–6 in `60 Status/Status 02 Retraction history.md`: the five gaps, grounding as `𝒮_t`-measurability, value versus
world learning, and "values only in acquired concepts". Rows 1–30 were carried verbatim from v5.

- It refers to a parent, `../persona-state-framework/`, which is **not** in this repository.
- It is superseded by the vault, and kept for the ideas the reviews dropped with their formulations. ROADMAP §6
  cites it as *SF* and says, gap by gap, what was retracted, what survives, and what is open.
- Its sha256 hashes are in `tools/frozen.json`; `tools/vault.py lint` rejects any change.
