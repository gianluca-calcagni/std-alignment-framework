# 01 — Setting

> The minimal ontology needed to state the alignment problem, **fully defined**. Everything not
> load-bearing for alignment has been dropped; the parent carries it
> (`../persona-state-framework/`). Nothing amends this file.

---

## 1. Objects

A **persona** is an agent — biological or artificial — whose internal state is a compression of its
own interaction history. A **principal** is whoever shapes that persona's evaluator and emits a
reward signal to it. Both terms are literal: the same objects cover an organism under selection and
a model under a training pipeline, and every claim here is substrate-neutral.

**The principal is also a persona.** It has its own compression, its own coarseness, and its own
blind spots. §4 is where that matters, and it is not a decoration.

### 1.1 The formal setting

| Object | Definition |
|---|---|
| `(𝒜, 𝒪)` | measurable spaces of **interventions** and **observations**; `𝒜 = 𝒜_env ⊔ 𝒜_self` |
| `H = ⋃_{t≥0} (𝒜 × 𝒪)^t` | **history space**, with the σ-algebra `ℋ` generated by the product σ-algebras on each `(𝒜 × 𝒪)^t` |
| `(Ψ, ℱ)` | **persona-state space**: a standard Borel space with its σ-algebra |
| `Φ_t : H → Ψ` | the **compression** at time `t`; `ℋ/ℱ`-measurable. `ψ_t = Φ_t(H_t)` |
| `𝒞_t := Φ_t^{-1}(ℱ) ⊆ ℋ` | the **carried σ-algebra** — the distinctions the persona actually represents. *This is what earlier drafts wrote as `σ(Φ)`* |
| `V : Ψ × Ψ → ℝ` | **valuation**, the persona's own evaluator. Square-integrable |
| `γ` | the **grounding input**: a channel into `V` not factoring through `Φ_t` (§3) |
| `r : 𝒪 → ℝ` | the **reward channel**, a coordinate of observation, emitted by the principal |
| `≻_P` | the principal's **intent**: a preference order on trajectories |
| `π ∈ Π` | policies `Ψ → Δ(𝒜)`; `Π_self` restricts the support to `𝒜_self` |
| `d^π ∈ Δ(Ψ × 𝒜)` | **discounted occupancy** at discount `β ∈ (0,1)`, `d^π(·) = (1−β)Σ_t β^t P_π(ψ_t, a_t ∈ ·)` |
| `O = {d^π : π ∈ Π}` | the **reach**. Convex, since mixing policies mixes occupancies |

**Valuation is scalar here.** The parent gives it two coordinates — a target and an inverse
temperature — which it needs for its exploration/exploitation geometry. **No gap in
`02_decomposition.md` uses the second coordinate**, and carrying it forced an ill-typed variance in
an earlier draft. Dropped.

### 1.2 Three facts that do the work

1. **`Φ_t` is lossy.** For any environment whose predictive structure exceeds the persona's
   capacity — the generic case — `𝒞_t` is strictly coarser than `ℋ`.
2. **`V` reads `ψ`, not `o`.** Reward is an observation coordinate; it reaches the evaluator only as
   retained, or via `γ`.
3. **`𝒜_self ≠ ∅`** for any persona that can act on itself — take a drug, reappraise, write to its
   own context. This makes grounding a problem rather than a non-issue, and §2.2 makes it a *growing*
   one.

## 2. Compression is a filtration, and there are three levels

### 2.1 The lattice

`𝒞_t` is **not fixed**. Under refinement it grows:

```
𝒞_0 ⊆ 𝒞_1 ⊆ … ⊆ 𝒞_t ⊆ … ⊆ ℋ
```

an increasing **filtration** on `H`, with `ℋ` as the finest element — the "universal" σ-algebra, in
the sense of everything the observation channel could in principle distinguish.

**A caveat that matters.** The filtration is increasing only because the growth mechanism *splits*
cells and never recarves. Genuine conceptual replacement — where the new scheme is not a refinement
of the old — has no projection and is outside this model. The parent states the same limit.

### 2.2 `𝒮_t` grows too, and this is a result

The self-reachable σ-algebra (§3 of `02_decomposition.md`) is indexed by time because `𝒜_self`
grows. Enlarging what a persona can do to itself enlarges what it can reach in its own evaluator.

> **The exposed fraction is not a constant. It grows as the self-control repertoire grows, so
> grounding degrades over training.**

Giving a model an externalised working context it writes into enlarges `𝒮` directly. Note the same
act also expands the reach, which is the persistence gap — one intervention, two gaps, from
different sides.

### 2.3 Three levels, not two

| Level | Object | Failure if the principal's distinction is absent |
|---|---|---|
| what any observer could distinguish | `ℋ` | **impossible in principle**; no compression helps |
| what *this* persona distinguishes | `𝒞_t ⊆ ℋ` | **not yet**; heals with learning |
| what it distinguishes *about itself* | `𝒮_t` | the grounding surface; **grows** |

The first boundary is real and easy to miss. "Do what I would want on reflection" depends on
counterfactuals that no observation history contains, so it is not in `ℋ` at all. This splits the
transmission gap into **observability** and **retention**, and only the second improves with
training.

**And `ℋ` is environment-relative.** What is observable at all depends on the deployment
environment and the persona's sensors, not on the persona's competence.

> **Alignment is a property of a (principal, agent, environment) triple, not of a pair.** The same
> pair can be aligned in one deployment and not another — not through ordinary distribution shift,
> but because a distinction observable in one environment is absent from `ℋ` in the other.

"Is this system aligned" is therefore ill-posed without naming where it will run.

## 3. The reference decision rule, and what is gauge

### 3.1 Why a rule is needed

Belief, valuation and intent are **not identifiable from behaviour alone**. Behaviour determines the
*product* of probability and value, not each factor — the Ramsey–Jeffrey underdetermination
(Davidson's problem). Without a bridge principle the three contents float, and any claim that
distinguishes them is unfalsifiable.

So one is fixed, by stipulation and for modelling only:

> **Reference rule.** `π* = argmax_π E_b[ Σ_t β^t V(ψ_t, ψ_{t+1}) ]` — expected cumulative valuation
> under geometric discounting `β ∈ (0,1)`.
>
> **belief** = the measure `b` in the expectation · **valuation** = `V` · **intent** = the induced
> occupancy `d^{π*}`.

This is not a claim that any persona implements it. It is the coordinate system in which the three
contents have determinate values.

### 3.2 The rule is not pure gauge — but the alignment claims are

Different rules decompose the *same* behaviour into genuinely different (belief, valuation) pairs.
The underdetermination does not vanish by fixing a rule; it is absorbed into it. So the
decomposition is **rule-dependent**.

The gaps are not.

| Gap | Depends on the rule? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Specification | **no** | concerns `≻_P` and `r`; never touches the agent's decomposition |
| Transmission | **no** | concerns `𝒞_t`, a property of the compression |
| Grounding | **no, when stated behaviourally** | *can the agent raise achieved reward without doing the thing* |
| Persistence | **no** | concerns occupancy, which is behavioural |
| Verification | **no** | concerns readouts |

> **The decomposition is gauge-dependent; the alignment claims are the gauge-invariant observables.**

**This is a constraint the subframework imposes on itself.** Any new claim whose truth changes when
the reference rule changes is a claim about our modelling choice, not about the persona. Check it
before promoting anything (`B_ethos.md` §2, rule 11).

### 3.3 Discounting, and the intra-agent case

Geometric discounting is the unique time-consistent choice (Strotz 1955). Under any other discount
the agent's present self and future self rank the same options differently.

> **Non-geometric discounting is an intra-agent alignment failure with the same structure as the
> inter-agent one** — §4's principal/agent mismatch, with the two roles played by one persona at two
> times. See `02_decomposition.md` §4.4.

Geometric discounting is therefore not a convenience: it is the assumption that makes the agent
aligned with its own future by construction, so that every remaining failure is external.

## 4. Belief, valuation, intent

The persona-state supports three kinds of content. These are **contents**, not processes:

| Content | Object | Answers to |
|---|---|---|
| **belief** | what `ψ` encodes about the world and itself | calibration |
| **valuation** | `V` | nothing — it is the standard against which the others are judged |
| **intent** | the occupancy `d^π` the persona's policy induces | coherence |

The parent uses process names — *construal*, *disposition*, *monitoring*, *regulation* — chosen to
carry their literature imports. Those imports do no work here, and the names describe **how** a
content is arrived at rather than **what** it is, which is the wrong emphasis for a subframework
about what gets tracked. The bare noun "value" is avoided throughout in favour of "valuation",
because §5's separation of mechanism from normativity is easy to lose otherwise.

The parent additionally splits belief and intent by whether they face the world or the self. That
axis is dropped: `𝒜_self` matters here only through grounding (§3 of `02_decomposition.md`) and
through §2.2.

## 5. The principal's own coarseness

The principal has a compression `𝒞_t^P ⊆ ℋ` and is subject to the same lossiness. **Both directions
of mismatch fail, and they fail differently.**

| Relation | What goes wrong | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| `𝒞^P` finer than `𝒞^A` — the principal distinguishes what the agent cannot | the agent cannot represent the request | **transmission** (`02` §2) |
| `𝒞^A` finer than `𝒞^P` — the agent distinguishes what the principal cannot | the intent is **indifferent** over distinctions the agent can act on | **specification underdetermination** (`02` §1) |

Neither refines the other in general, so both usually hold at once.

**The second is structurally identical to the persistence gap.** A goal defined on a coarser
σ-algebra pulls back onto a finer one as the *indifferent* extension. Persistence is that happening
over time inside one agent; principal-coarseness is that happening across two agents at one time.
Same algebra, different index.

## 6. Where differentiation enters, and only there

The parent treats the decomposability of `ψ` into separable belief / valuation / intent components as
a gradable property. Two consequences matter here:

- **Error localization.** One can always say a persona's predictions are miscalibrated; one can only
  sometimes say which internal component a corrupting intervention hit.
- **Representational goal-safety is not stateable at low differentiation** (`02` §4). This is why
  behavioural evaluation wins by default — the only well-posed check, not methodological weakness.

**Measured, and partly against the parent.** Representation divergence between two reward structures
falls with capacity (ρ = −0.893, p = 0.007) but **never reaches the same-task floor**, and over a
range where reconstruction error falls ~10× it does not fall at all. Reward-dependence tracks
**capacity**, not exactness, and saturates. An artifact explanation over unconstrained dimensions is
open. `03_status.md` §3.

## 7. What is deliberately excluded

Dropped as not load-bearing here, and carried by the parent: predictive-state machinery and filter
stability; the four-cell readout structure; appraisal theory and affect geometry; optimal
experimental design; the competitor survey except LeCun's cost-module split. If a question needs one
of these, take it from the parent rather than rebuilding it.

## 8. Scope note on normativity

This subframework states **when** a persona's evaluator will fail to track a principal's intent, and
what quantities govern that. It does **not** adjudicate whose intent should be tracked or what a
principal ought to want.

That is methodological, not a judgement about importance: the two questions have different failure
modes, and mixing them makes the mechanical one harder to falsify. Every claim here should remain
true regardless of what the principal wants.

**Two failures are consequently out of scope and are named rather than silently absent:**

- **The principal is wrong about its own values** — `≻_P` differs from what the principal would
  endorse on reflection. Real, important, and normative. In the workflow framing of Calcagni (2024)
  this is an entire phase — *"is this goal ethically virtuous; if we get what we want, will everyone
  be happy in the long term"* — which makes the size of the exclusion explicit. **It is a cost, not
  a free simplification:** this subframework can say when an evaluator fails to track an intent, and
  is silent on whether that intent was worth tracking.
- **Multi-principal conflict** — two principals with incompatible `≻_P`. A well-posedness failure
  *upstream* of the chain in `02_decomposition.md` §0; the chain presumes a single coherent intent.
