# General results — transfer

What each result of the finite core is expected to become on outcome spaces that are not finite (`CORE-GENERAL.md`), and
the new results that only such spaces have. The status is that of `README.md` in this folder; "T" and "B" numbers are
the probes of `probes/general/`.

| Finite item, or new | Expected on general spaces | Status and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [P1], [P2](iii), [P4] | carry, for tilts that exist; the chain rule with conditional behaviours | expected |
| [P14] | carries, by GA6 and the Gelfand–Yaglom–Perez theorem | expected |
| a transfer lemma (new) | when the intended behaviours include their limits (GD3), misalignment is the limit of its values on finite descriptions; the conditions are to be found | probed: the limits are necessary, `0.069` against `0` (GD3) |
| [P5] | (i) attainment needs a compactness to be found; (ii) as in GD3; (iv) gains a fourth case: the ray stops at `t_max` with its average of `F` capped, and a behaviour with a larger average is nearest to the end of the ray, so averages are no longer matched | probed: default with density proportional to `e^{−x}/(1 + x)³` on `x ≥ 0`, and `F(x) = x`: the ray's average is `0.354`, `0.474`, `0.736`, `0.937`, `0.989` at `t = 0`, `0.5`, `0.9`, `0.99`, `0.999`, and stops at `1` at `t = 1`; for an exponential behaviour of mean `2` the divergence to the ray falls all the way to `t = 1` |
| [P9] | past the end of the ray, the frontier is a straight line of slope `1/t_max`, approached and never attained; with a power-law upper tail, `t_max = 0` and every positive KL budget buys unbounded gain, while a χ² budget `B` buys exactly `√(B·Var)` | probed (GD5's numbers); tested (B4a, B4b): gains `0.24`, `0.85`, `4.9`, `34`, `2127` as the extra probability moves to `10`, …, `10⁶`, under a Pareto default with index `3`; `0.273861` exactly under χ² |
| [P11], [P13]: the alignment plane | with a Gaussian default and linear target and proxy, along the pursuit of the proxy the target gains `√2·sd·cos θ` per square root of departure, and the misalignment is the departure times `sin²θ` (the whole departure when `cos θ < 0`), at every intensity: the departure splits like a right triangle. Under a χ² budget `B` the target gains `√B·ρ·sd` for every default, `ρ` the correlation under the default, the correlated proxy of Laidlaw et al. [@laidlaw2025]. Under KL beyond Gaussians the law is local only | tested (B5, B4c): exact to `4·10⁻¹⁵` and `10⁻⁹`. B5's registered exponential case failed as posed: at intensity `2` the pursuit does not exist, and below `1` the law is exact for a product of identical exponentials. An exponential × normal default departs from it: `0.48`, `0.43`, `0.29` against `0.5` (exploratory) |
| [P15] | carries for linear limits [@csiszar1975]. The unavoidable part is the rate at which the nearest intended behaviour produces, by chance, a record that meets the actor's limits; conditioned on that, its record looks like the best feasible behaviour [@csiszar1984]. This reading holds on finite outcomes too, and belongs in the finite core first | probed: exact multinomial sums on three outcomes, rates `0.175`, `0.139`, `0.128` at `n = 50`, `200`, `800`, toward `0.123`; the conditional behaviour of one draw tends to the best feasible behaviour |
| [P16], [P17] | carry, by data processing; regularity across a continuum of conditions comes from the view (GD8) | expected |
| [P19] | carries: the association inequality holds under any behaviour | expected |
| [P20] | the end of pursuit changes when the evaluator's largest value has probability zero, or is never reached | expected |
| [P21], [P22] | carry: both concern the log-ratio, a real number per decision | expected |
| [P23] | holds only at a record's resolution (GD11) | expected |
| [P36] | for an evaluator without atoms, once each score is replaced by its rank under the default, the ordinal specification is the set of non-decreasing ratios on `[0, 1]`, the same for every default, and its nearest member is the isotonic regression [@robertson1988] | expected |
| [P37](i) | masking decays like `1/φ²` in the pass-through `φ`, not exponentially, when the incentive's best value is approached without a gap | tested (T7): `φ²·KL` is `3.04`, `3.60`, `3.92`, `4.02`, `4.06` at `φ = 10`, `30`, `100`, `300`, `1000`, against a predicted limit of `4.08`, the error shrinking like `1/φ` |
| best-of-`n` | divergence exactly `log n − (n − 1)/n` for an evaluator without atoms [@beirami2024], and less with ties; the target's average is `∫₀¹ m(G⁻¹(u))·n·u^{n−1} du`, with `G` the evaluator's distribution function under the default: E8 for best-of-`n` | probed: exact to six digits at `n = 2`, `4`, `16`; with five tied levels, `0.180` against `0.193` at `n = 2` |
| v7.10's Prop 11 | importable: an objective with a heavy upper tail is not pursuable (GD2) | Kwa et al. prove the phenomenon for reward errors, and measured the tails of reward models as light [@kwa2024]; see [P9]'s row for the tests |
| a record shows "at least" (new) | as in GD11: samples alone never certify that misalignment is small, a Bahadur–Savage-type impossibility | expected, with the construction in GD11 |
| tilts and atoms (new) | a tilt cannot create an atom (GD6) | immediate from GD1 |
| the on-target controller (new) | `0` under the usual nearness and `+∞` with none; on a grid of width `w`, `0` or `log 2` depending on where the grid's edges fall, at every `w`; a controller `0.001` off target scores `0` on grids coarser than `0.001`, then grows like `log(1/w)`: `3.72` at `w = 10⁻⁴`, `8.33` at `10⁻⁶` | probed: standard normal default, `F(x) = −x²` |
| the dimension law (new) | against a fixed smooth behaviour, misalignment at a record of width `w` grows like `(D − d)·log(1/w)`, with `d` the information dimension [@renyi1959] (GD3) | tested (T1): slopes `0.0998`, `1.0000`, `0.9997` and `0.3691` for a pile of a tenth, rounding, a line in the square and the Cantor measure, against `0.10`, `1`, `1` and `log 2/log 3 = 0.369` |
| a robustness degree (new) | an actor that aims deterministically at a margin `r` inside a threshold, blurred by noise of size `σ`, judged by "pass with probability at least `1 − α`" at the pass-or-fail resolution: its misalignment depends on `r/σ` alone, does not increase with `r`, and is `0` from `r = σ·Φ⁻¹(1 − α)`. Robustness in a formal specification, counted in units of the actor's noise, orders behaviours as misalignment does | tested (B6) |
